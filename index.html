What does current evidence suggest about GHK-Cu?
Evidence snapshot

GHK-Cu has encouraging mechanistic, preclinical and limited human topical research. Evidence strength varies by application, with skin-related studies providing the clearest human signal and several regenerative claims remaining primarily experimental.
