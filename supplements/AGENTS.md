# Supplement Profile Instructions

These instructions apply to English supplement profiles under `supplements/`. Equivalent localized pages inherit this contract through their locale manuals.

## Evidence contract

- Identify the nutrient, compound, form, and relevant formulation distinctions.
- Separate evidence for deficiency correction from claims in replete populations.
- Separate human randomized, human observational, preclinical, in vitro, mechanistic, and theoretical evidence.
- State population, formulation, comparator, endpoint, duration, and limitations when material.
- Prefer primary trials, systematic regulatory or public-health sources, and official nutrient reference sources.
- Distinguish biological plausibility from demonstrated clinical outcomes.

## Safety boundary

- Describe established adverse effects, interactions, contraindication context, and tolerable upper limits only from authoritative sources and without turning the page into an individualized regimen.
- Do not prescribe doses, schedules, combinations, treatment protocols, or purchasing choices.
- Do not imply that “natural” means safe or that biomarker improvement guarantees clinical benefit.
- Material safety or regulatory changes require qualified human review.

## Page requirements and handoff

Use the supplement checklist in `docs/CONTENT_PAGE_CONTRACT.md`. Unsupported sections use `N/A` or `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`. QA validates structure and links; SEO & i18n validates metadata and locale identity; a qualified human approves material scientific publication.
