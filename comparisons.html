Comparison contract

Every future comparison should answer six questions

01

Mechanism

Which receptors, pathways or biological targets differ?

02

Evidence

How much is human, preclinical, in vitro or mechanistic?

03

Population

Who was studied, for how long and against what comparator?

04

Outcomes

Which endpoints were observed and which remain inferred?

05

Safety

What is known, unknown and formulation-specific?

06

Regulation

Is the compound approved, investigational or preclinical?
