Development model

From hypothesis to clinical adoption

Not every molecule follows the same path, and progression does not guarantee approval.

1

Discovery

Biology, target identification and early hypothesis generation.

2

Preclinical

Cell, tissue and animal studies.

3

Human trials

Safety, exposure and controlled efficacy research.

4

Regulatory review

Formal evaluation for a defined indication.

5

Adoption

Post-approval evidence, guidelines and real-world use.

Regulatory context

Registration, approval and availability are different concepts

R

Registered trial

A planned or active study, not authorization.

A

Approved indication

Authorization applies to a defined use and jurisdiction.

C

Commercial availability

Availability alone does not establish quality or approval.

P

Post-market evidence

Long-term effectiveness and uncommon risks may emerge later.
