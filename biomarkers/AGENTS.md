# Biomarker Profile Instructions

These instructions apply to English biomarker profiles under `biomarkers/`. Equivalent localized pages inherit this contract through their locale manuals.

## Interpretation contract

- Define the analyte, specimen, measurement context, units, and biological role.
- Distinguish reference intervals, clinical decision thresholds, risk associations, diagnostic criteria, and treatment targets.
- State that intervals and assays may vary by laboratory, method, population, age, sex, pregnancy status, and jurisdiction.
- Separate human randomized, human observational, preclinical, in vitro, mechanistic, and theoretical evidence.
- Do not describe association as causation or a surrogate change as a guaranteed clinical outcome.
- Prefer official laboratory, guideline, regulatory, and primary-study sources.

## Safety boundary

- Do not diagnose from a value, prescribe testing frequency, recommend individualized treatment, or instruct readers to change medication or supplementation.
- Urgent or critical-value language must come from an authoritative source and receive qualified human review.
- Explain uncertainty, confounding, biological variation, and assay limitations proportionally.

## Page requirements and handoff

Use the biomarker checklist in `docs/CONTENT_PAGE_CONTRACT.md`. Unsupported fields use `N/A` or `EVIDENCE_UNAVAILABLE`. QA validates structure and links; SEO & i18n validates metadata and locale identity; a qualified human approves material clinical interpretation.
